About URI

Where students become researchers.

The Undergraduate Research Initiative (URI) – Suez Canal Branch is a student-driven initiative that aims to empower undergraduate students to engage in scientific research, academic activities, and community projects.

URI provides students with practical experiences, research training, leadership opportunities, and professional networking to help them build a strong academic and research foundation.

Everything you need to know before starting your URI journey.

URI is a student-driven initiative focused on undergraduate scientific research, academic activities, leadership, and community projects.

Undergraduate students interested in research, academic development, leadership, or student activities can explore the available URI paths.

SRT stands for Scientific Research Training. It provides practical training that introduces undergraduate students to scientific research.
